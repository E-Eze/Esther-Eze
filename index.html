About

Over the past five years, I’ve worked across nonprofits, technology ecosystems, and early-stage organizations. My work has included designing fellowships, administering grants, supporting research, managing partnerships, and building the operational foundations that keep projects moving.

I’m especially interested in how institutions work: how people coordinate, how resources are allocated, and how technology changes what’s possible.

That curiosity also shapes my learning. I’m developing skills in software development, data analytics, and financial modelling, alongside studying economics, finance, and mathematics. I’ve co-designed courses in history, technology, and business, and curated learning playlists to make these subjects easier to explore.

And curated learning playlists in economics, finance and mathematics—tools I see as essential for understanding large-scale coordination and institutional design.

Archivi.ng

2020–2025

Operations Manager

Archivi.ng is a nonprofit working to make Nigerian history accessible to everyone, everywhere. I joined as one of its earliest employees and helped establish the systems supporting its research and programs.

My work included:

  • Designing and managing the first Archivi.ng Fellowship, supported by Luminate funding.
  • Coordinating researchers, contractors, and collaborators across research, technology, and communications.
  • Managing participant communications, contracts, budgeting, and payments.
  • Conducting historical research for Fear No Man, a documentary podcast produced by Higher Ground.
  • Writing archival essays, including a piece exploring Nigerian women’s historical influence and political significance.

Faces of Web3

2021–2023

Founder & Editor

During a sabbatical from Archivi.ng, I began exploring the challenges facing African artists and creators, and whether emerging technologies could offer new ways to organize, fund, and distribute their work.

Faces of Web3 became a space for that exploration through writing, research, education, and community organizing.

DreamCatchers Academy

Naija Flood Aid

Explorations

Some questions have led to smaller research and writing projects.

Worlding a New Browser

Research into the browser as an interface for collective intelligence, drawing on Douglas Engelbart’s Open Hyperdocument System, Alan Kay’s work, and Marshall McLuhan’s Understanding Media.

Credit & Digital Identity Research

An exploration of credit infrastructure in Nigeria that expanded into digital identity and migration verification. I examined whether these systems could reduce barriers facing Africans seeking opportunities across borders, then stepped away after considering the surveillance risks they could introduce.

Working Woman (Substack)

An independent writing project where I research businesses and ideas, connect what I learn, and publish the resulting insights.
